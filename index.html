What / why

Built because pixel art tooling shouldn't need an install.

PixisEditor is a browser-based pixel art editor: layers, frame animation, a dockable tool/panel UI, PNG/GIF export, all running client-side on Canvas 2D. Ships as a single-file build, so it works the same whether it's hosted, downloaded, or opened straight from disk.

It's an independent project developed solo, currently in active beta with new tools and fixes shipping regularly.

renderer Canvas 2D, tiled compositor
stack vanilla ES6, Vite single-file build
runs entirely client-side, no server
status open beta, shipping weekly

What's new

Recent updates

1.32.4 - 2026-08-10

  • Right-click almost any button now offers Edit Hotkey..., and dozens more menu/panel/toolbar commands (File menu, panels, color, text, layers, animation, brush & stamp presets, atlas, process recorder) can finally be assigned a key from Settings -> Hotkeys.
  • Every panel now has a "..." menu next to its title bar for quick access to common actions - toggle header, hide/show its own toolbar, fit to content, edit hotkey, restore defaults - and those actions stay reachable from the right-click menu even when a panel's toolbar is hidden.
  • Dragging a stamp or a tile from the Atlas browser onto the canvas: hold Shift while you drop it to place it on a brand-new layer instead of the one you're currently on.
  • Selection outlines and the colour-range hover preview no longer lag or stall on large canvases.
  • Fixed animation frame-list rows staying the wrong size until you resized the window, when a neighboring panel changed height.

1.29.0 - 2026-08-07

  • The top bar shows your project name (default Untitled) with a star when unsaved - double-click or F2 to rename.
  • File -> New project... starts fresh after a warning if you have unsaved work, then asks for a name.
  • Selection outlines no longer paint a full-canvas gold frame on empty exterior; marquee and lasso keep solid geometry for fill/clip.
  • Settings -> Canvas pan & zoom: Invert zoom flips wheel and drag zoom direction.

1.28.7 - 2026-08-06

  • The Delete key now always deletes your selection, even if you've reassigned the customizable shortcut to something else - your custom shortcut still works too.

1.27.3 - 2026-08-05

  • Select, Lasso, and Colour Range now share one transform frame - switch between them without losing your selection; move, resize, and rotate work with any of those tools active.
  • Hold Alt or Ctrl while moving a selection to flip it horizontally or vertically (live preview, applied on release).
  • Marquee and lasso replace/add keep only solid (opaque) pixels, so empty holes are dropped from the selection.
  • Fixed free-transform bugs: enlarging no longer leaves paint outside the frame; resize and flip on a rotated selection keep the angle; undo, blur, tool switch, and edit shortcuts no longer leave a half-finished move or resize stuck.
  • Shift-add and Ctrl-subtract on the marquee again use the full selection shape (not only painted pixels), and starting those modifiers from inside the frame draws a new marquee instead of moving.
  • Trackpad two-finger horizontal swipe on the canvas undoes/redoes (same idea as the touch three-finger swipe); toggle in Settings -> Gestures.
  • Fixed Split View: a second pointer on the other pane no longer corrupts zoom and pan of the pane you are drawing on.
