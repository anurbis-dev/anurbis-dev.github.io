Privacy

Last updated: 2026-07-24 · Applies to pixis.ink and the hosted demo/beta builds.

What we collect

The editor runs entirely in your browser. Project files, pixels, and drawings stay on your device unless you export or save them yourself. We do not operate a backend that stores your art.

Analytics

To understand traffic and improve the site, we may use:

  • Umami — privacy-friendly page views and custom events (referrer, country/region aggregate, device type, button clicks). No advertising cookies; no personal profiles for ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity (if enabled) — session insights such as clicks, scrolls, and heatmaps. Clarity may use cookies or local storage; see Microsoft Privacy.

Analytics providers process data as independent controllers under their own policies. We do not sell personal data.

Beta signup

If you apply for beta testing, the email you submit is processed by Formspree and delivered to the project maintainer so we can send a beta link. We do not use that address for unrelated marketing.

Third-party links

Links to GitHub, Discord, X, and similar services are governed by those services’ privacy policies.

Contact

Questions: open an issue or reach out via the contacts on github.com/anurbis-dev.
