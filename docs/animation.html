Animation

A multi-track frame timeline that any layer can link into, named frame actions for logical clips (walk, idle, attack…), parallel tracks that composite together at the same play step, and a transport with Forward / Reverse / Ping-pong playback.

The panel

Two layers, four frames — a named action ("Walk") covering the last three, Frame 3 held for 3 units instead of 1. New projects start with one track; add more by dragging frames into new lanes (see Parallel tracks).

Frame toolbar

Add / Duplicate / Delete frame, and 🔗 Link — links every selected paint layer (folders expand to their nested paint layers) to every selected frame; turns red ("unlink") once the whole selection is already linked.

2
Frame actions bar

＋ creates a named action from the current selection; 🏷 assigns/unassigns frames to the active one. Chips scope Play/export to just their frames — see states below. Sprite frame blocks can be inserted here by dragging a sprite layer (Canvas-as-sprite) from the Layers panel onto a frame; a modal lists named actions from the sprite's target canvas — pick one to insert that action's frames as a live-linked, locked block showing the sprite's sub-frame behavior synchronized to the host timeline. No named actions on the target canvas → its whole timeline inserts directly as the block, no modal. Each block also gets its own chip here (one per block instance, even for repeated copies of the same action) — click to jump to its frames. Block frames show the sprite's own image, only their eye is editable (no opacity/duration), highlight in the block's own color, and move/delete together as a whole (dragging or deleting any one member moves/removes the whole block) so each member stays aligned with its source frame; each member is its own timeline cell, same as a regular frame.

3
Frame cell

👁 visibility, thin top bar for opacity (drag), a colored dot when the frame belongs to an action, drag the cell to reorder (Shift+drag to copy), duration at bottom-left (cell width spans that many columns), linked-layer count top-right. Drag the left/right edge handles to stretch or shrink hold duration.

4
Duration

Hold units at the current FPS (1–999, default 1). The cell stretches across that many timeline columns; later frames on the same track are pushed so holds never overlap. Frame 3 here is set to 3 — it stays on screen 3× longer during playback and looks three slots wide.

5
Linked-layer count

Click selects those layers in the Layers panel; Ctrl+click unlinks every layer from the frame(s) in one step. Frame 1 shows 2 (both layers), the rest show 1 (just the layer active when they were added).

6
Transport

⏮/⏭ step through visible frames; ▶ plays/stops; the mode button cycles Forward → Reverse → Ping-pong.

7
FPS

Playback speed, 1–30 (default 6) — multiplied by each frame's own duration to get its hold time.

Frame-action chips, at a glance:

AllNo scope — Play/export use the full timeline
●Named, active — scoped to just this action's frames
◐Folded — every member frame hidden from the list (re-click to bring back)

Parallel tracks

The timeline is not a single strip — each track is a horizontal lane of frames bound to a global timeline column (slots may be empty on some tracks). At a given play/export step, every visible track contributes its frame at that global column; those frames composite together on the canvas.

Create & remove tracks
  • Drag a frame above or below a track lane, or onto the gap between lanes, to place it on a new track.
  • A track disappears when its last frame is moved away (there is always at least one track left).
  • Locked tracks reject drops and frame edits from that lane.
Track headers (left side)
  • Sticky column beside the frame lanes: track name (double-click to rename), color swatch, eye (hide track from play/composite; Ctrl+click isolates like Layers), and lock (same icon styles as Layers).
  • Drag the vertical separator left (~12px) to collapse the headers into a thin reopen tab; drag open or click the tab to restore.
Scrub & resize
  • Left-drag on empty timeline content (ruler, empty slots, gaps — not on a frame cell): relative scrub from the current frame at the click point (no need to line up with the playhead).
  • Shift + middle-drag or Shift + wheel — scrub over frames too (panel or canvas).
  • Middle-drag (no Shift) — pan the timeline freely; content is not pinned to the left/top edge.
  • F over the Animation panel — fit all tracks and frames to the current window size.
  • Ctrl/Alt + right-drag on a track: vertical scales height (Shift = all tracks); horizontal scales shared slot width (zoom under cursor).
  • Scrub ruler is fixed at the top or bottom of the timeline (Settings → Timeline scrub → position) — it does not scroll with tracks. Track headers stay locked to their lanes.
  • Play-range markers fill the lanes viewport height and only move with horizontal scroll; color / ruler height / position live in Settings → Timeline scrub. Playback never moves vertical scroll; cannot-play toasts name the reason.
  • Invert, speed, and ease match Settings → Canvas pan & zoom (same feel as canvas drag-zoom).
Play & export
  • Playback and GIF/PNG sequence export step by column across all visible tracks (hidden tracks are skipped).
  • Duration for a step is the longest duration among the frames in that column.
  • Frame actions still scope which frames participate within each track.
Composite
  • Selecting a frame (or the playhead) composites every parallel frame at the same column on other visible tracks — not only the active one.
  • Tracks and frame placement are saved with the project file.

Playback modes

Click the mode button (next to ▶) to cycle. Needs at least 2 project frames, and at least one visible frame with a linked layer, to start playing at all.

→ Forward
  • Default. Plays frame 1…N, loops back to 1.
← Reverse
  • Plays N…1, loops back to N.
↔ Ping-pong
  • Plays forward to the end, then backward to the start, repeating — no jump cut at the loop point.
Playing a named action
  • Select just that chip (plain click when nothing else is selected, or Ctrl+click to solo it) before pressing ▶ — Play/export follow the active scope, not the full timeline.

Gestures & shortcuts

Frame cell
  • Drag the top opacity bar — set this frame's opacity (Shift applies to selected / whole run)
  • Drag the cell — reorder within the lane, or above/below a lane / onto a gap to create or join another track
  • Shift+drag — drop a copy instead of moving
  • Drag across several eyes — mass-toggle visibility
Linked-layer badge
  • Click — select those layers in the Layers panel
  • Ctrl+click — unlink every layer from the selected frame(s)
Frame-action chip
  • Click — solo this action (or, if it's the only one selected already, toggle fold/unfold)
  • Shift+click — add/remove from the play/export scope
  • Ctrl+click — exclusive solo (disables every other action)
  • Ctrl+Shift+click — add to the solo set without dropping the others
  • Hold (~500ms) — delete the action (member frames stay, just unassigned)
  • Drag — reorder the chip strip
Keyboard
  • P — play / stop
  • ← / → — previous / next visible frame
  • Add / duplicate / delete frame, frame actions, and the mode cycle have no default hotkey — toolbar/pointer only.
